Dough

Recipe for 1 person / 4 people

95 / 380 g water
0.06 / 0.22 g instant yeast
2-3 / 9-12 g salt
153 / 610 g flour
Mix.
Perform the first stretch and fold after 30 minutes.
Perform the second stretch and fold after another 30 minutes.
18h
Cover and let rise at room temperature. Divide into
250 g
large dough balls.
Optional 24–48h
let mature in the refrigerator.
3h
Let rest again before baking.
Shape with a raised crust, add toppings, and bake for approx. 1 minute at
450–480 °C
Briefly cool on a wire rack and serve.
