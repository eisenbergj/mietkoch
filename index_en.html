In a nutshell

A relaxed get-together with your loved ones where you'll learn to make the perfect gourmet pizza quickly and safely while having a great time.

  • Duration: approx. 3 hours
  • Participants: 8–12 people
  • Focus: Practical baking + basics
  • Prior knowledge: none required
  • Included: Ingredients, oven, tools, beverages, travel and recipe materials
  • Language: English (German on request)
  • Location: I come to you with a large kitchen required. Tübingen and surrounding areas
  • Price: from €40 per person

Course Content

  • Independent preparation (and take-home) of dough
  • New York Style and Napoletana
  • Direct, indirect, cold and warm management techniques
  • Shaping dough into pizza and topping
  • Launching, baking and turning pizza
  • Biochemical processes and their effects
